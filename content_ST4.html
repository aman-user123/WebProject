Reducing Carbon Footprint

Small daily changes can make a big difference. By walking or biking instead of driving, reducing meat consumption, and using reusable bags and bottles, individuals contribute to a lower environmental impact. Simple lifestyle changes such as walking more, reducing meat consumption, and cutting down on single-use plastics help reduce our overall carbon footprint.

Promoting Clean Energy

Using renewable energy sources like solar, wind reduces dependence on fossil fuels. Installing solar panels at homes supports clean energy policies. Bioenergy is becoming more accessible and affordable, enabling cleaner energy solutions even at local levels.

Climate Change Science

Climate change is happening; it is caused mostly by humans; and it creates many serious and damaging effects. Human activities have been the main driver of climate change, primarily due to the burning of fossil fuels like coal, oil and gas. These come from using gasoline for driving a car or coal for heating a building, for example. Clearing land and cutting down forests can also release carbon dioxide.
